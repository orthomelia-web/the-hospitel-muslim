How we check these guides

  • We walk the routes. Travel times are door-to-door from the villa, not station-to-station.
  • We never blur the labels. Certified halal (with the certifying body named), Muslim-owned, and Muslim-friendly are three different things, and each listing says which one it is.
  • We re-check every three months. Small restaurants in Tokyo open and close quickly, so each guide carries the date it was last verified — and closed places are removed, not quietly left in.
  • Tell us if something has changed. info@thehospitel.jp
