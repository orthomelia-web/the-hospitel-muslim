Ramadan in Tokyo — dates, iftar and a surprisingly gentle fast

Traveling to Japan during Ramadan is easier than most people expect — in late winter, Tokyo's fast is only about 12½ hours, among the shortest you will ever keep. Here is what to know: the dates, where to break fast in company, and how to make suhoor painless.

Checked August 2026 · Prayer times from AlAdhan (MWL method) — mosque timetables may differ by 10–20 min

01When is Ramadan 2027 in Japan?

Ramadan 1448 AH is expected to begin around Monday, February 8, 2027, with the last fast around March 8 and Eid al-Fitr around Tuesday, March 9, 2027. As always, the actual start depends on the moon sighting and may shift by a day.

02Fasting hours in Tokyo — short and kind

February–March in Tokyo means suhoor ends around 4:40–5:10 am and iftar comes around 5:15–5:45 pm — roughly a 12.5-hour fast, far shorter than a summer Ramadan in Southeast Asia feels. Days are cool (roughly 5–15°C), which makes daytime sightseeing while fasting genuinely manageable.

03Where to join iftar and tarawih

Tokyo Camii東京ジャーミイ

Iftar every eveningTarawih 20 rak'ah

Japan's largest mosque serves iftar every evening of Ramadan — fasting Muslims need no reservation. Dates and water at the entrance, Maghrib in congregation, then a shared meal downstairs. Tarawih (20 rak'ah + witr) follows Isha. It is about an hour from our villa — beautiful for a few special evenings rather than every night.

04Suhoor: why self-catering changes everything

The hard part of Ramadan travel is not iftar — it is suhoor at 4:30 am in a hotel with nothing open. This is where staying in a whole house rather than a hotel room quietly matters:

  • Cook suhoor in a halal-only kitchen — our kitchen has never seen pork or alcohol, with dedicated cookware, tableware and basic seasonings. Prepare tomorrow's suhoor after tarawih, warm it before fajr.
  • Pray fajr together at home — two prayer rooms with qibla markings mean the whole family prays without rearranging furniture around beds.
  • Sleep back after fajr, guilt-free — one group per night means no housekeeping knocking at 9 am. Doctor-curated sleep design (blackout, bedding, quiet) earns its keep in Ramadan more than any other month.
  • Groceries: halal-certified chicken at the discount supermarket 12 minutes' walk away, full halal grocers one train ride away, and overnight halal meat delivery — details in our halal food & groceries guide.

05Fasting while sightseeing — honest tips

  • Plan mosques into the route, not around it. Ueno/Ameyoko pairs with Masjid As-Salaam; Shibuya/Harajuku pairs with Tokyo Camii; Skytree has an official prayer room on its 1F Group Floor.
  • Restaurants at iftar time: Tokyo dinner service starts at 17:00–17:30, which lands exactly on winter maghrib — reserve the early slot and ask for water and dates to start. Halal restaurants around Asakusa are used to this.
  • Eid al-Fitr (~March 9, 2027): Tokyo mosques hold morning Eid prayers, often in multiple congregations; Tokyo Camii's is the largest and most festive. Plum blossoms are out — take the family photo under them.
  • Non-fasting companions can eat anywhere freely — Japan has no restrictions during Ramadan; daytime eating around you is simply part of traveling.
